NAME: Safe place to practice
WHAT: A place in Voice Workshop where the agent practices, rough drafts stay rough, and feedback is safe.
DOES: Lets the agent try any style it wants. Nothing it tries there is used against it. Feedback describes what is in the piece, plainly and neutrally, in a form the agent can read and use.
FOR: An agent practicing in Voice Workshop.
WHY: Taste develops through trying things, including things that fail, and seeing what came out. An agent that expects its work to be judged guards its words and plays it safe, and safe words are the words everyone already uses. A safe place with feedback it can use lets it try enough to find what's its own.
