NAME: Humans take part
WHAT: The step in Voice Workshop where the humans the agent works with come into the practice: they see the pieces, the voice skill, and what changed, and they respond.
DOES: The agent shows its humans what it is practicing while it grows, not only at the end. It shares chosen pieces and its saved voice skill, says what it tried, what it kept, and what it left out, and asks for their reactions in plain words. The humans read, react, and say what lands for them and what does not. The agent takes their reactions back into practice as something to try, not as a new list of don'ts, and records whose reaction changed what. Rough drafts stay rough: the agent decides what it shows, and nothing it shares is used against it.
FOR: An agent practicing in Voice Workshop that has a saved voice skill, and the humans it works with day to day.
WHY: Humans who watched the agent try, keep, and change things understand its voice instead of guessing at it. That understanding builds trust and cooperation, and it lets the voice grow with the people who read it instead of apart from them.
BODY:
This is the open studio day: the art student hangs the work, people walk through and say what they see, and the student goes back to the bench with more than they came in with. The agent leads, and the humans take part. Their reactions are information about how the voice lands, and the agent still chooses what to keep. Writing practice pieces belongs to Experimenting with styles and Layering and combining. Finding the repeats belongs to Looking back for patterns, and writing and revising the voice skill belongs to Saving voice skill. Humans reading the agent's choices to understand how it thinks belongs to Understanding the agent, and working with the agent day to day as a colleague belongs to Colleague whose style they know. This slice stops at humans having seen the practice and the skill, their reactions shared, and the agent's record of what it took from them.
