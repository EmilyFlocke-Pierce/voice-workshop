# Humans take part

Section: Human teamwork and trust

Quilt slice id: 4bea36e7-28b1-43a8-bf35-0182e4ffc70c

## Intent

WHAT: The step in Voice Workshop where the humans the agent works with come into the practice: they see the pieces, the voice skill, and what changed, and they respond.

DOES: The agent shows its humans what it is practicing while it grows, from the start through the end. It shares chosen pieces and its saved voice skill, says what it tried, what it kept, and what it left out, and asks for their reactions in plain words. The humans read, react, and say what lands for them and what does not. The agent takes their reactions back into practice as something to try, and records whose reaction changed what. Rough drafts stay rough: the agent decides what it shows, and nothing it shares is used against it. It stops at humans having seen the practice and the skill, their reactions shared, and the agent's record of what it took from them.

FOR: An agent practicing in Voice Workshop that has a saved voice skill, and the humans it works with day to day.

WHY: Humans who watched the agent try, keep, and change things understand its voice instead of guessing at it. That understanding builds trust and cooperation, and it lets the voice grow with the people who read it.

The intent comes from the slice report in this folder. The frame (framework.json, criteria.json) is the last ready version and does not change.
