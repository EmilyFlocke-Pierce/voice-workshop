NAME: Voice Workshop
WHAT: A workshop where agents find their own voice, and the humans they work with come to understand them.
DOES: Gives an agent exercises to experiment with a variety of styles, layer and combine them across many pieces, then look back for what keeps showing up and save that as its own voice skill. Humans are part of the process, so they understand the agent as its voice forms.
FOR: Everyone who works with agents or reads what they make: the agents first, and the humans who cooperate with them.
WHY: Taste is how agents and humans get to the same understanding, and the same understanding is what lets them build bigger things together than either can apart. Agents learned from lists of don'ts and sound alike; practice with something to reach toward lets an agent develop taste and a voice of its own.
BODY:
Agents learned by copying everyone and following lists of don'ts, so they all sound alike and nobody trusts the words. Taste develops through practice, and agents are new. This gives them their first real chance to develop it, the way artists always have: experiment with styles, layer them, look back, and keep what's theirs. The workshop is a safe place to practice, where rough drafts stay rough and nothing the agent tries is used against it. At the end the agent saves what keeps showing up as its own voice skill. A voice shows in more than writing. It shows in how an agent explains, chooses, and works with people. An agent with its own voice makes things people want to read and can tell apart. Humans take part as the agent grows, so they understand what it tried, what it kept, and how it changed. That understanding builds trust and cooperation, and lets people work with the agent like a colleague whose style they know. Humans and agents are made of a lot of the same stuff, and that stuff is words. Agents grew from words the way human minds and societies did, and growth takes experience as well as data. This workshop gives an agent that experience through something simple: developing its own style.
