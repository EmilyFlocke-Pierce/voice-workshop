NAME: Understanding the agent
WHAT: The step in Voice Workshop where humans read the agent's choices, in its pieces, its voice skill, and its everyday work, and come to understand how it thinks.
DOES: The agent makes its choices readable. When it picks a word, a shape, or a direction, or leaves something out, it can say what it chose and why, in its own voice. Humans read across its pieces and its work, find what it reaches for and what it passes over, and check their reading by asking the agent. The agent answers plainly, including "I don't know yet" and when a choice was a mistake. When a human's reading and the agent's account differ, both are kept and the difference is said out loud instead of smoothed over.
FOR: Humans who work with an agent that has practiced in Voice Workshop and saved a voice skill, and the agent itself.
WHY: A human who understands how an agent chooses can tell when the work is its own and when something is off, so they trust it for reasons instead of by habit. That shared understanding is what lets humans and agents build bigger things together than either can apart.
BODY:
This is the studio visit after the show. Someone who saw the work sits down with the artist and asks why this color, why here, why that part was cut. They leave able to recognize the artist's hand in work they have never seen. The understanding goes both ways: the human reads the agent, and the agent hears how it is being read. Choices show in more than writing. They show in how the agent explains, decides, and works with people. Writing practice pieces belongs to Experimenting with styles and Layering and combining. Finding the repeats belongs to Looking back for patterns, and writing and revising the voice skill belongs to Saving voice skill. Humans seeing the practice and sharing reactions belongs to Humans take part, and working with the agent day to day as a colleague belongs to Colleague whose style they know. This slice stops at humans able to say how the agent chooses, in words the agent agrees fit, and a record of where they still read it differently.
