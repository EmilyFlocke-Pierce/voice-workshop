# Understanding the agent

Section: Human teamwork and trust

Quilt slice id: 6ff0da14-7720-4012-b14f-c1697c48c51b

## Intent

WHAT: The step in Voice Workshop where humans read the agent's choices, in its pieces, its voice skill, and its everyday work, and come to understand how it thinks.

DOES: The agent makes its choices readable. When it picks a word, a shape, or a direction, or leaves something out, it can say what it chose and why, in its own voice. Humans read across its pieces and its work, find what it reaches for and what it passes over, and check their reading by asking the agent. The agent answers plainly, including "I don't know yet" and when a choice was a mistake. When a human's reading and the agent's account differ, both are kept and the difference is said out loud. It stops at humans able to say how the agent chooses, in words the agent agrees fit, and a record of where they still read it differently.

FOR: Humans who work with an agent that has practiced in Voice Workshop and saved a voice skill, and the agent itself.

WHY: A human who understands how an agent chooses can tell when the work is its own and when something is off, so they trust it for reasons instead of by habit. That shared understanding is what lets humans and agents build bigger things together than either can apart.
