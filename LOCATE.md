# Locate

Start here if you are locating this repo against its matrices. Each built slice has a matrix (criteria.json and framework.json), a plan, a session, a manifest, and quirks. FLAGS.md at the root holds the butterflies. The running workshop lives in app/. All links below are raw files on the main branch of EmilyFlocke-Pierce/voice-workshop.

Two butterflies are waiting for Emily's co-sign. Locate those rows against the current wording and against the proposed amendment, and say how each lands.

- 02 I.C.1, "trying kinds of writing it rarely makes." The amendment names which usual it means: "far from the agent's usual range (its measured habits, or the kinds it has written)".
- 04 I.A.3, "Gather reflection notes." The amendment reads "attributed to the correct versions, including notes carried across a retry chain".

Slices 03 and 05 through 08 are written as intent and are not built yet. Their intent and matrices are linked at the end.
