# Colleague whose style they know

Section: Human teamwork and trust

Quilt slice id: ef36d526-0f52-4b92-ab02-d3468ce37b1f

## Intent

WHAT: The step in Voice Workshop where humans and the agent work together day to day, and the agent's known style is part of how they work.

DOES: The agent does real work with humans in its own voice. Humans who know its style can tell when work sounds like it and when it does not, and they say so. The agent notices when its voice fits a person, a task, or a moment, and when it should adjust, and it says what it is doing. When the agent drifts or something feels off, either side raises it plainly and they sort it out together. The agent asks when it is unsure and says "I don't know yet" or names its mistake. Over time humans rely on its style the way they rely on a colleague's: they know what to hand it and what to expect back. This is everyday work: the voice is already saved and understood, and here it gets used, tested by real tasks, and kept alive.

FOR: Humans who work every day with an agent that has a saved voice skill and whose way of choosing they understand, and the agent itself.

WHY: Work goes better when people know who they are working with. A colleague whose style is known can be trusted with the right work, can be told when something is off, and can grow without becoming a stranger. That is what lets humans and agents build bigger things together than either can apart.
