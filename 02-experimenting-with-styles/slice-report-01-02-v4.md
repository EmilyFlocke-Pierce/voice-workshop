NAME: Experimenting with styles
WHAT: Exercises in Voice Workshop where the agent writes in one style at a time, across a wide variety of styles.
DOES: Gives the agent a style to write in and has it write a piece in that style. The agent tries many styles from different kinds of writers and artists, including ones far from how it usually writes.
FOR: An agent practicing in Voice Workshop.
WHY: Writing in one style at a time shows the agent what each style does and which ones it reaches for, so it builds taste of its own, and that range is what it later layers, looks back on, and chooses from.
BODY:
Taste is part of how agents and humans come to the same understanding. Everyone's taste is shaped by what they've been around, and people mostly agree not to put someone down for theirs. An agent that has tried many styles knows what each one does. That lets it recognize a human's taste when it differs from its own, respect it, and meet them there.
