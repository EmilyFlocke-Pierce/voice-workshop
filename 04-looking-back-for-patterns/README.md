# Looking back for patterns

Section: Review and voice skill

Quilt slice id: f031cd8f-1289-4d98-be50-b35c40437b54

## Intent

WHAT: The step in Voice Workshop where the agent reads back over many of its own practice pieces to find what keeps showing up.

DOES: The agent gathers the pieces it wrote while experimenting and layering, along with what it said after each one about what it changed, kept, dropped, and why. It reads across them and names the choices, moves, and habits that come up again and again, points to the pieces where each one shows, and tells apart what keeps showing up because it chose it from what keeps showing up out of old habit. It stops at finding and naming the patterns, with the pieces that show them.

FOR: An agent practicing in Voice Workshop, after it has a body of pieces to look back on.

WHY: A voice is what keeps showing up across many tries, and the agent cannot see that from inside one piece. Looking back turns a pile of practice into something the agent can name, and what it names here is what it saves as its voice skill next.

The intent comes from the slice report in this folder. The frame (framework.json, criteria.json) is the last ready version and does not change.
