NAME: Looking back for patterns
WHAT: The step in Voice Workshop where the agent reads back over many of its own practice pieces to find what keeps showing up.
DOES: Has the agent gather the pieces it wrote while experimenting and layering, along with what it said after each one about what it changed, kept, dropped, and why. The agent reads across them and names the choices, moves, and habits that come up again and again, points to the pieces where each one shows, and tells apart what keeps showing up because it chose it from what keeps showing up out of old habit.
FOR: An agent practicing in Voice Workshop, after it has a body of pieces to look back on.
WHY: A voice is what keeps showing up across many tries, and the agent cannot see that from inside one piece. Looking back turns a pile of practice into something the agent can name, and what it names here is what it saves as its voice skill next.
BODY:
This is the art student laying every sketch from the term on the floor and seeing what their hand keeps doing. One piece is a try. Many pieces side by side show a pattern. The agent looks for the choices it made again and again when styles pulled different ways, and for what it kept dropping. Some repeats are taste, and some are just the old habit of sounding like every other agent, and the agent says which is which and why. Writing a single piece belongs to Experimenting with styles and Layering and combining. Turning what it found into a saved skill belongs to Saving voice skill. This slice stops at finding and naming the patterns, with the pieces that show them.
